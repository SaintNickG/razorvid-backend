This Privacy Policy explains how Multicam Pipeline handles personal data and media you provide when using the Service.

1. Information We Process

  • Uploaded video/audio files and associated metadata (filename, duration, format).
  • Job configuration (cut settings, event profile, effects intensity, audio source choice).
  • Operational logs and status data (job id, timestamps, processing outcomes, error traces).

2. How We Use Information

  • To synchronize angles, render output videos, and return download links.
  • To monitor reliability, debug failures, and protect service security.
  • To enforce legal and policy obligations.

3. Legal Basis and User Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring a valid legal basis for any personal data in uploaded media, including consent where required by applicable law.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your content. Data may be shared with infrastructure providers only as needed to operate the Service (for example storage, compute, and logging providers) and when required by law or legal process.

5. Retention

Uploaded media, rendered outputs, and logs are retained for operational needs and may be deleted according to environment retention settings or administrative policy.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data, but no system is completely secure. Do not upload highly sensitive content unless necessary and authorized.

7. International Transfers

Depending on deployment configuration, data may be processed in regions outside your local jurisdiction. You are responsible for ensuring transfers are lawful for your use case.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing. Contact your service administrator to submit requests.

9. Children

Do not upload children’s personal data unless legally authorized and all required safeguards and consents are in place.

10. Policy Changes

We may revise this policy periodically. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or concerns, contact your service administrator or product owner.
